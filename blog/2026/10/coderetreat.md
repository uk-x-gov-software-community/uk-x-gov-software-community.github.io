Aysun Aliosman (Home Office) and David Heath (GDS) are organising a Coderetreat for people who build software across government, as part of [Global Day of Coderetreat](https://www.coderetreat.org/).

Global Day takes place on 13 and 14 November 2026, bringing software developers together around the world for a day of deliberate practice. 

A Coderetreat gives you space away from delivery pressures to work on the fundamentals of software development, experiment with practices such as test-driven development, and learn alongside other developers.

You don’t need to bring a presentation or arrive with a finished solution. Bring your curiosity and be ready to practise, pair and share what you learn.     

## Event details

The Coderetreat will take place on 13 November 2026, from 10:00 to 16:00 at the Government Digital Service office in London. Registration is free but required to secure your place.

[Join the event on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/cross-government-code-retreat-tickets-1997450526294) 

## Who should come?

The event is for software developers across government who want to make time for focused practice and connect with colleagues facing similar challenges. You can take part whether you’re new to Coderetreat or have joined one before.

## Organisers and facilitators

Aysun Aliosman (Home Office) and David Heath (GDS) are organising the event. They will also be facilitating the Coderetreat sessions, ensuring a supportive environment for all participants.   

We hope you can join us. [Find out more about the Coderetreat format](https://www.coderetreat.org/about/) and look out for the local event details here or contact us on hello@uk-x-gov-software-community.org.uk and we will pass on to the organisers.
