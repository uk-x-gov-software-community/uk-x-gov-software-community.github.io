Our Annual General Meeting (AGM) will take place on **Thursday 10 December 2026**. Full joining details and the agenda will be sent to members on the mailing list ahead of the day.

It's been a big year for the community. We've run monthly Lean Coffees, supported our special interest groups, and brought people together in person at GovDev North in Newcastle and GovDev Midlands in Nottingham. None of that happens without a group of volunteers behind the scenes, and the AGM is where we elect the people who'll take that work forward next year.

## We're looking for facilitators

We're inviting **self-nominations** for the management committee, the small group of facilitators who help run the community. Under our [Terms of Reference](/tor/), committee members serve a one-year term, and the committee includes these roles:

- **Chair** – chairs management committee meetings and sets the strategic direction
- **Secretary** – organises committee meetings and makes sure actions are recorded and shared
- **Infrastructure officer** – looks after the website, GitHub organisation and mailing list, and acts as our data protection officer
- **Communications officer** – manages the mailing list, blog and newsletter
- **Social media officer** – grows our presence on social media
- **Events officer** – curates our events programme, including Lean Coffee, and works with the SIGs

You don't need to be a senior leader or a seasoned organiser to stand. We want a committee that reflects the breadth of our community: hands-on developers, associated technical roles, leads, career changers, and people from departments and arm's length bodies of every size. If you care about software engineering in government and want to help others connect, learn and share, we'd love to hear from you.

## How to nominate yourself

The nomination form is now available on the [nominations page](/nominations/). We'll also share the link on the mailing list and in [`#software-development`](https://ukgovernmentdigital.slack.com/archives/C23NQUH3L) on cross-government Slack. 

Nominees will have their nomination information shared on our website for members to review ahead of our AGM on 10th December. In a change from last year, we are proposing that all nominees are elected as a slate at the AGM. This reflects the intention of the committee to allow anyone who wants to be more involved to join the management committee.

## Thinking about it? Let's talk

Putting yourself forward can feel like a big step, so please don't let uncertainty hold you back. If you'd like to talk through what's involved, how much time it takes, or whether it's right for you, get in touch with me directly. I'm happy to have an informal chat.

I'm sure the rest of the current committee would be just as happy to talk, so feel free to reach out to whoever you feel most comfortable with. You can also contact us via the [shared mailbox](mailto:cross-government-software-engineering-community-organisers@digital.cabinet-office.gov.uk) or find us on Slack.

We look forward to seeing you at the AGM, and hopefully to reading your nomination.
